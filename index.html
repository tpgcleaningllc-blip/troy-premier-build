Frequently asked questions

How do I get a price?
Use the estimate form above, pick a service, answer a few questions about your home, and see an estimated price instantly.
What areas do you serve?
Troy, MI and the surrounding Metro Detroit communities, including Royal Oak, Birmingham, Rochester Hills, Sterling Heights, and Warren.
Can I set up recurring cleaning?
Yes, choose weekly, every 2 weeks, or monthly on the form and your discount is applied automatically to your estimate.
How does payment work?
Residential bookings are paid securely through Stripe when you submit the form. Commercial jobs are quoted first, then paid through a link in your client portal.
Where do I check on a booking or pay an invoice later?
Use the Client Portal link in the menu above. Sign in with the same email you booked with and you will see your bookings, jobs, and any open invoices.
